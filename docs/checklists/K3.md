# K3 · Kanban extras: manual checklist

Install: no new packages. On start the app database gets migration 0012 (repeating tasks and links
from tasks to Search & BI). **Back up `backend/data/ncode.sqlite` first.** Then `pytest`, `npm test`,
`npm run lint`, `npm run build`, `npm run e2e`.

## 1. Fixes from your review

- [ ] Hover hints: open a board's *More* menu with the mouse and close it, or tap a button on the
      phone; no hint pops up afterwards. Resting the mouse on a button still shows its hint after 3 s.
- [ ] A task's description and comments show exactly what you typed (no `**`, `-` tricks, no tip
      under the field); a web address in them is a link.
- [ ] Board settings in Russian: *Скрыть доску* without the text in brackets; the buttons do not overlap.

## 2. The "done when": a weekly check

- [ ] On a board, a task "Weekly safety walk" due next Monday, with two steps, a person and the
      scrap dashboard linked (*Search & BI → Link a dashboard or question*).
- [ ] *When → Repeats → Every week*. The task shows *Every week*.
- [ ] Tick the steps, move it to *Done*: the note says the next one is due the Monday after; a new
      "Weekly safety walk" is in *To do* with the same person, the link and unticked steps.
- [ ] *Undo* in the note: the new one goes away, the task is back where it was and still repeats.
- [ ] *Calendar*: the task is on its Monday; drag it to Tuesday, the due date changes (*Undo* works).
- [ ] *My tasks* (the person's own login): the task is under *Next 7 days* with its board; *Done*
      there moves it and makes the next one too.
- [ ] Click the dashboard's name on the task: Search & BI opens that dashboard.

## 3. Calendar

- [ ] *Board | Calendar* at the top of a board; the filters (My tasks, Late tasks...) apply to both.
- [ ] Late tasks are red, finished ones ticked; more than three on a day: *+N more* lists them all.
- [ ] *No date* on the right: drag one onto a day to give it a due date.
- [ ] On a phone: a list of days (late first, then today), the switch in its own row, nothing cut off.

## 4. My tasks

- [ ] *My tasks* on the boards page shows *2 late* in red when you have late tasks; also in the boards
      menu at the top.
- [ ] Groups *Late · Today · Next 7 days · Later · No date · Done this week*. Tasks on boards you are
      not a member of show without *Done* and *Move to…*.
- [ ] Opening a task shows it on the same page; closing it returns to the list.

## 5. Links and permissions

- [ ] Someone who may not open the linked dashboard sees "A dashboard you cannot open" on the task,
      not its name; they cannot link it themselves.
- [ ] Removing a link and linking a question are in the task's history.

## What I verified here

- Backend: Kanban tests incl. My tasks (boards, hidden boards, removed and long-finished tasks), the
  dates of repeats (month ends, weekends, done early and three weeks late), a weekly task coming back
  with its people, labels and steps, no copies when moved back and forth, links following each
  person's permissions; migration 0012.
- Frontend: unit tests for the calendar (weeks from Monday, the phone list, month and day names in
  both languages) and for My tasks groups; browser tests for the calendar (drag, Undo, no date, phone),
  My tasks (Done, Undo, opening in place), a weekly task with Undo, linking and opening a dashboard,
  and the hint behaviour (the phone test fails with the old hint code).
- Left for later: a timeline view, board templates, notifications.
