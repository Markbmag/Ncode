# K2 · Ncode - Kanban on the phone: manual checklist

Install: no new packages and no migration; rebuild the frontend (`npm run build`, or
`docker compose build`). With Docker, the new `Caddyfile` sends the service worker and the manifests
without caching. To open the app **without a connection** the phone needs HTTPS: with
`NCODE_TLS=internal`, install Caddy's root certificate on the phone once
([install guide → Phones](../guide/install.md)). Over plain `http://` everything else below works,
except opening offline.

## 1. The "done when" (iPhone)

- [ ] In Safari open Ncode, sign in, open Kanban → Share → *Add to Home Screen*: the name is
      **Kanban** and the icon is teal. Open it from the home screen: full screen, no Safari bars.
- [ ] On a board: tap the camera button next to *+ Add a task*, take a photo. A task "Photo 14:05"
      opens with the photo and the name selected; type a name, add a step.
- [ ] Close it, press and hold the task, let go → *In progress*. The tab *In progress* is now
      selected and a note offers *Undo*.
- [ ] Open the task, tick the step: "1 of 1 steps done".
- [ ] Press and hold the task and drag it onto the *Done* tab at the top (it lights up green).
- [ ] At no point did the page zoom in or need pinching, also while typing.

## 2. Phone layout

- [ ] Tabs show every stage with its count; swiping changes the selected tab, tapping a tab
      scrolls there. The board name shows in full (two lines at most).
- [ ] A quick tap opens a task; only press-and-hold moves it. Scrolling the list of tasks still works.
- [ ] On Android, picking up a task gives a short buzz.
- [ ] *Search & BI* added to the home screen from a BI page is a separate indigo **Ncode** icon.

## 3. Photos

- [ ] A photo from the camera (about 3-5 MB) arrives as about 1 MB; it looks right way up on the
      phone and on a computer. *Add photo or file* inside a task makes big photos smaller too.
- [ ] A PDF or a small picture is sent unchanged.

## 4. Weak or no connection

- [ ] Airplane mode, then open the Kanban icon: the app opens with *No connection* at the top (not
      the sign-in page). Airplane mode off: the strip goes and the boards load by themselves.
- [ ] Leave the app for a few minutes (another app, screen off), come back: the green dot turns
      green again and changes made meanwhile by others are shown.
- [ ] Signing out, then opening offline, shows the sign-in page (nobody stays signed in by mistake).

## 5. No regressions

- [ ] On a computer the board looks as before (no stage tabs, no camera button); dragging with the
      mouse works. The K1 checklist still passes.

## What I verified here

- Frontend 301 unit tests (stage in view, which photos are made smaller, photo task names, both
  manifests and icons, the service worker run in a sandbox: it answers pages, built files and icons
  and never `/api` or `/ws`, and without a network gives back the saved app), lint, build.
- Browser 52 tests, including the done-when on an iPhone 13 screen with real touch events: a task
  from a photo, named, a step, held and moved to *In progress*, ticked, dragged onto the *Done* tab,
  then checked on the server (in Done, step ticked, photo with preview).
- On the built app: the service worker installs and keeps the app; opening with the network off
  shows the boards page with *No connection*; back online it loads by itself. A 4032×3024 photo
  (1.7 MB) arrived as 631 KB with a preview.
- Not verified here: a real iPhone and Android phone (Safari's own behaviour, the camera, the
  home-screen icon) - the emulated tests use Chromium with the iPhone's screen and touch.
