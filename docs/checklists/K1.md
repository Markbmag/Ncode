# K1 · Ncode - Kanban: manual checklist

Install: in `frontend`, `npm install` (adds the drag-and-drop library @dnd-kit). On start the app
database gets migration 0010 (Kanban tables); uploaded files go to `backend/data/kanban/` and are
part of backups. **Back up `backend/data/ncode.sqlite` first.** Optional setting:
`NCODE_KANBAN_MAX_UPLOAD_MB` (15).

## 1. Launcher

- [ ] After signing in: two tiles, **Ncode Search & BI** and **Ncode - Kanban**. Pick Kanban, close
      the tab, open Ncode again: it goes straight to Kanban. The ⊞ button (Kanban top bar, BI sidebar)
      switches between the two. Old BI links still work.

## 2. A board (plain words, no dragging needed)

- [ ] **+ New board**: name and description; it starts with *To do · In progress · Done*. Everyone
      in the company can see it; add members (people or groups) who may change it.
- [ ] **+ Add a task** in a stage; open it: what, who (several people), when (due date shows
      "Due tomorrow" / "3 days late"), how important (Low · Normal · High · Urgent), labels.
- [ ] **Steps**: add three, tick one → "1 of 3 steps done" with a bar, also on the board.
- [ ] **Add photo or file**: from a PC, and from a phone camera. The photo shows as a picture.
- [ ] Comment; open *History*: who did what, when.
- [ ] Move a task with **Move to…** and by dragging; **Undo** in the note brings it back.
- [ ] **Remove from the board** asks first and can be undone; hidden tasks can be brought back.
- [ ] *My tasks*, *Late tasks*, *More filters* (person, label, priority), search.

## 3. Together (the "done when")

- [ ] Three people open the same board: two computers and a phone. Moves, ticked steps and comments
      appear for the others within a second, with a note "carol moved …" and the author and time in
      the task's history.
- [ ] Someone who is not a member sees the board but cannot change it.

## 4. Phone

- [ ] On an iPhone (Safari): stages swipe sideways, a task opens as a full page, buttons are easy to
      tap, no zooming needed. (Adding Kanban to the home screen as an app comes in K2.)

## 5. Languages and themes

- [ ] Everything in Russian (задачи, этапы, шаги) and in English; dark and light theme.

  Done, step counts, comments, attachment type and size checks, download headers, backup, history,
